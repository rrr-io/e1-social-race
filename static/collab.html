Counts valid ENHYPEN voting proofs sent by partner fandoms. A proof is valid when ENHYPEN is the last image of the Mnet Plus certificate, the partner's own image is where it should be, and the certificate ID has not been counted before. Anything that cannot be verified with certainty waits in the Review tab.
